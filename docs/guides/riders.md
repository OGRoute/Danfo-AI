# For riders

You do not need a wallet, an account, or any crypto to use Danfo as a rider.
Open the app and ask.

## Ask a question

Type or tap the mic and speak. Danfo understands **Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Nigerian
Pidgin, and English**, and answers in the language you asked in.

> _"Mo fẹ lọ si Oshodi lati CMS"_
> _"How much be from Yaba go Ikeja?"_
> _"Ina son zuwa Berger daga Ojota"_

You get the route, where to change danfo, and a fare estimate. Tap the speaker
to hear it back in a Nigerian-accented voice.

## What you see

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chat** | Route answers grounded in the community knowledge base, not invented |
| **Mic** | Speak your question instead of typing |
| **Map** | The route and its stops drawn on the Lagos map |
| **⭐ Corrections feed** | Recent community corrections and their status |
| **History** | Your past questions, so you can re-check a route |

## How much to trust the answer

Fares and routes are **community-maintained and approximate**. Danfo answers
from data that contributors staked money on and peers voted through — that is a
much stronger signal than a random listing, but it is not an official timetable,
and Lagos traffic makes any estimate a range rather than a promise.

Two things worth knowing:

* **Fares move fast.** Fuel prices, rain, and peak hours all shift them. A fare
  verified last month may be stale.
* **Anything in the feed marked Pending has not been verified yet.** It is a
  claim someone staked on, not an accepted fact.

## Found something wrong?

That is the entire point of the project. If a fare or route in an answer is
wrong, you can correct it — and get paid when peers confirm you were right.
See [For contributors](contributors.md).

## Privacy

Your questions are sent to an inference endpoint to be answered, and to the
speech service if you use voice. Chat history is stored so you can revisit past
answers. Nothing you ask is written to the blockchain — only corrections you
explicitly submit go on-chain, and those are public by design.
